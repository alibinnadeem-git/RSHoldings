Privacy.

This page explains the current handling of information entered into the RS Holdings website.

Current inquiry form

The current website does not transmit inquiry information to an external recipient. The inquiry interface prepares information for the visitor to review and copy locally.

Future contact services

If an official electronic submission service is enabled in the future, this privacy notice should be updated before personal information is transmitted or stored.

Information visitors may enter

The inquiry interface may request a name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, property or investment interest, indicative investment range and a free-text message.

Third-party services

The website may load technical libraries required for visual presentation. No statement on this page should be interpreted as a substitute for the privacy policies of any third-party service subsequently introduced.
